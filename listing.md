## Main store listing

**Full description:**
WowCity Seller is a free billing, stock and GST app made for clothing and fashion shops in India. Our team sets up all your products for you, and we support you 24/7.

BILLING IN SECONDS
• Scan barcodes with the phone camera or a scanner
• Line and bill discounts, held bills and estimates
• Split payment: cash, UPI, card and credit (udhaar) in one bill
• Returns and credit notes in a few taps
• Share bills on WhatsApp or print A4 and thermal bills, with a UPI QR for any balance due
• Keeps billing even when the internet is down, and syncs when it's back

STOCK YOU CAN TRUST
• Stock by item, size, colour and store
• Stock value at cost and MRP
• Adjustments with reasons, transfers between stores, and full item history

PURCHASES AND LABELS
• Fast purchase entry with sizes side by side, GST inclusive or exclusive
• Supplier payments and balances
• Barcode price labels on A4 sheets or 50×25 mm rolls

MONEY AND DUES
• Customer and supplier dues with statements
• Polite WhatsApp payment reminders in one tap

23 READY REPORTS, GST INCLUDED
• Sales, returns, fast and slow movers, staff performance
• Stock, movement and dead stock
• GSTR-1, GSTR-2 and GSTR-3B with CGST, SGST and IGST split
• Profit and loss, dues ageing and more. Print any report or export it to Excel

STAFF AND STORES
• A separate login for each staff member, with roles, store access and working hours
• Many stores in one account, each with its own bill series

GET FOUND BY SHOPPERS NEARBY
• Choose products to show on the free WowCity shopper app
• Shoppers see the design, price and that it's in stock, then walk into your shop
• Your cost price, suppliers and profit are never shown

FREE, WITH FULL SUPPORT
No setup fee and no monthly fee. Share your stock list, an Excel sheet or photos of your register, and our team adds every product for you. Call, WhatsApp or email us any time.

Works on Android, iPhone and iPad (in the browser), and on any computer. Learn more at https://luzzan.com
